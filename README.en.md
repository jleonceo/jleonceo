## Featured projects

- [Sales, from a raw file to a dashboard](https://github.com/jleonceo/de-datos-en-bruto-a-decisiones): three
  business questions answered with clean data tested with statistics, published on
  [a website](https://de-datos-en-bruto-a-decisiones.vercel.app). Python, pandas and statistics.
- [Lead scoring](https://github.com/jleonceo/lead-scoring-ml): calling the top 20 % reaches half of the buyers. A
  model ranks contacts by their probability of buying. Python, scikit-learn and XGBoost.
- [Customer segmentation](https://github.com/jleonceo/RFM-Customer-Analytics): campaigns aimed at the customers most
  likely to buy again. It groups customers by how recently, how often and how much they buy. Python and pandas.
- [Cash forecasting](https://github.com/jleonceo/tesoreria-forecast-ia): one month ahead its average error is
  €4,334, against €29,444 for the average of previous years. It forecasts cash month by month and measures its
  error against the past. Python.
- [Accounting fraud detection](https://github.com/jleonceo/control-interno-fraude-ia): duplicate payments, gaps in
  numbering and suppliers with no trace, flagged across the whole ledger for a person to review. Python.
- [Accounting with AI agents](https://github.com/jleonceo/accounting-agent-swarm): an invoice goes in and its
  accounting entry comes out proposed and checked, for a person to review before it is recorded. Four agents read
  the documents, propose, check and reconcile the bank. Claude.

## What I do

- Data analysis and BI with Power BI, DAX, Power Query, star schemas, SQL on MySQL, Python and pandas, so that
  management sees at a glance where to act and every department works with the same figure.
- Accounting under the Spanish chart of accounts, closing, bank reconciliation, VAT, cash forecasting and internal
  control, to close sooner and correct deviations from budget before they cost margin.
- Machine learning with scikit-learn and XGBoost, time series and model validation, to turn the company's history
  into useful forecasts.
- Applied AI with Claude and Claude Code: agents, connecting AI to company systems (MCP, n8n), language-model
  evaluation and personal-data protection. It lets a company delegate multi-step tasks with a person supervising.
